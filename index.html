Frequently Asked Questions

Where are physical found items stored?

Valuable items (wallets, electronics, keys) reported to campus security are safely stored at the Main Security Desk in the Student Union (Room 102).

How do I verify ownership of an item?

When clicking "Claim Item", you will be asked to describe distinguishing features (e.g. wallpaper picture, serial number, card initials, key chains) or provide verification answers.

What happens if an item is not claimed for a long time?

Unclaimed items are safely kept for up to 90 days. Unclaimed electronics and IDs are dispatched according to university policy.
